# Pitch Deck Outline (7 slides)

## Slide 1 — Title
SisterStop
*Your calm, connected ride home.*
Team names · Hackathon name · DevelopHER Track

## Slide 2 — The problem
"After a late hospital shift, Maya waits alone at a station after her train is delayed. She is not necessarily in immediate danger — but she feels isolated, uncertain, and stressed."

- Nontraditional work schedules create difficult commute windows.
- Safety concerns and anxiety compound during delays.
- Existing transit tools rarely combine peer connection and emotional support.

## Slide 3 — The solution
1. **Connect** — Virtual Buddies
2. **Protect** — Safety Hub and comfort audio
3. **Calm** — AI-guided grounding support

## Slide 4 — User journey
```text
Plan trip -> Match with a buddy -> Delay or anxious moment ->
Cover call / grounding -> Arrival check-in
```
Use real screenshots from the deployed app.

## Slide 5 — How it works
```text
Auth0       -> Trusted account access
Gemini      -> Contextual, structured support
ElevenLabs  -> Natural voice check-ins and grounding
Tiger Data  -> Anonymous time-series community pulse
```

## Slide 6 — Responsible design
- Opt-in matching
- Route/time matching, not precise public location tracking
- Aliases and mutual consent
- Block/report/leave controls
- AI does not make emergency decisions
- Official MARTA and emergency pathways stay visible

## Slide 7 — Impact and future
**Today:** a prototype for safer, calmer late-shift transit experiences.
**Next:** transit data integration, employer partnerships, accessibility testing, multilingual support, moderated verification.

> "No one should have to choose between getting to work and feeling supported on the way home."
