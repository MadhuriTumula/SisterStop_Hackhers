# Product Context

## Product name
SisterStop

## Tagline
Your calm, connected ride home.

## Core statement
SisterStop is a privacy-conscious safety and well-being companion for people commuting during late-night and nontraditional work hours.

## User problem
Late-shift riders can experience uncertainty, isolation, stress, service delays, and discomfort while traveling. Existing transit and safety tools may not offer peer connection, a calming in-the-moment experience, and practical safety resources in one supportive flow.

## Solution
SisterStop combines:
- Opt-in peer connection through Virtual Buddies
- AI-guided Calm Mode for short, structured support
- Voice-based comfort and grounding tools
- Official safety-resource routing
- Optional anonymous community demand insights

## What makes this different
The product does not claim to solve transit safety alone. It helps riders create a support layer around a commute through consent-based matching, practical coping tools, and clear escalation pathways.

## Primary user journey
A late-shift worker selects a route and departure window, finds an opt-in peer companion, receives support during a delay or stressful moment, uses a grounding or comfort-audio tool, and completes a safety check-in upon arrival.

## Hackathon MVP constraints
- Use seeded data for matches.
- Do not store real sensitive information.
- Do not use real-time geolocation for public matching.
- Do not expose precise rider locations.
- Demonstrate one complete polished journey.
