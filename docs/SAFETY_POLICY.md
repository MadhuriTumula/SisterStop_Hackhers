# Safety and Privacy Policy

## Product boundary
SisterStop is a peer-support and well-being prototype. It is not an emergency service, dispatch system, medical provider, security service, or substitute for official transit safety channels.

## Urgent-risk behavior
When a user mentions immediate danger, violence, stalking, assault, medical emergency, self-harm, or being followed:
1. Display a clear urgent-help state.
2. Tell the user to call 911.
3. Show the Safety Hub.
4. Never claim that the app has contacted anyone.
5. Do not ask for more detail than necessary.

## Match privacy
- Use aliases only.
- Show route, station zone, and time-window overlap.
- Never show home address, exact GPS location, phone number, legal name, or detailed schedule.
- Exact meeting details should remain fictional or user-controlled in the prototype.
- Users can leave, block, or report a match at any time.

## Audio rules
- Do not simulate police, emergency dispatch, or official MARTA communications.
- Do not clone an identifiable person's voice.
- Label all generated audio as a comfort tool or guided support.
- Do not use audio as a substitute for emergency action.

## AI rules
- Gemini should return concise, supportive, structured UI content.
- The AI should not make legal, medical, or emergency decisions.
- The AI should not diagnose anxiety, trauma, or danger.
- The AI should never promise privacy beyond what the product can support.

## Demo disclaimer
Show this in the app footer and demo:
"SisterStop is a hackathon prototype for peer support and well-being. It does not replace 911, MARTA Police, or official transit safety services."
